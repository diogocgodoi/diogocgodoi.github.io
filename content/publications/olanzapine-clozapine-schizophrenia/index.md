abstract: "Background: Treatment-resistant schizophrenia remains a major challenge in psychiatric treatment. Although clozapine is the gold standard therapy, its adverse effect profile has led to interest in alternatives such as high-dose olanzapine. Methods: PubMed, Embase, and Cochrane databases were systematically searched for randomized controlled trials comparing high-dose olanzapine with clozapine in treatment-resistant schizophrenia. Results: Five studies involving 469 patients were included. No statistically significant differences were observed between high-dose olanzapine and clozapine on the Clinical Global Impressions Scale or PANSS Positive scores. High-dose olanzapine was associated with significant improvements in PANSS Negative scores. Hypersalivation and postural hypotension were more frequent with clozapine, while weight gain was similar between groups. Conclusions: High-dose olanzapine showed comparable efficacy to clozapine for most outcomes and a potential advantage for negative symptoms, with fewer reported adverse effects in several domains."

summary: "A systematic review and meta-analysis comparing the efficacy and safety of high-dose olanzapine and clozapine in treatment-resistant schizophrenia."
