## Description

My research interests also extend to understanding how the brain generates functions such as perception and language. I am particularly interested in how neurological conditions can affect daily function, and how non-invasive brain stimulation can be used to treat dysfunction at a neural level. Some of the projects I have been involved in this area include:

* Linguistic Effects of tDCS in Primary Progressive Aphasia (1)
* Non-invasive Brain Stimulation in Primary Progressive Aphasia (2)
