## Description

I am interested in evaluating the effectiveness of interventions for mental health and neurological conditions. In particular, using meta-analytic approaches to synthesise evidence across studies and evaluate the effectiveness of psychological, pharmacological, and neuromodulatory interventions. I am also interested in evaluate the risk of different interventions at a population level.This sort of research can clinicians identify the most optimal treatment for their patients, and for regulatory bodies to create evidence-based guidelines.

Some of the main projects I have been involved in this area include:

* Acceptance and Commitment Therapy for Insomnia: An Updated Meta-Analysis (1)
* Non-invasive Brain Stimulation in Primary Progressive Aphasia (2)
* Non-invasive Brain Stimulation for Neuropathic Pain (3)
* High-Dose Olanzapine vs. Clozapine for Treatment-Resistant Schizophrenia (4)
* Atomoxetine for Cognitive Deficits in Stimulant Use Disorder (5)
* Association of Prenatal Acetaminophen Exposure With Risk of ADHD and ASD in Offspring (6)
* Risk of dementia in patients treated with anticholinergics for overactive bladder syndrome (7)
